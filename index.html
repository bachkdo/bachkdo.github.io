About Me

My research focuses on making expensive engineering analysis and design more data-efficient, reliable, and interpretable. I am especially interested in Gaussian processes, Bayesian optimization, uncertainty-aware learning, and inverse problems arising in structures, materials, and scientific computing.

Beyond methodology, I care about building tools that engineers can actually use: optimization frameworks that work with simulators, multi-fidelity pipelines that reduce cost, and data-driven methods that remain grounded in physical structure.

Research Highlights

Bayesian optimization

Thompson sampling, acquisition design, exact and scalable inner-loop optimization, and robust optimization under uncertainty.

Multi-fidelity methods

Surrogate models and optimization strategies that combine cheap and expensive information sources to cut computational cost.

Inverse problems in mechanics

Parameter identification, reliability, and data-assisted modeling for structural and constitutive problems.

AI for engineering design

Scientific machine learning and data-driven design methods that connect optimization with physical insight.
